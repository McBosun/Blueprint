Blueprint Terms and Community Rules

Last updated: 30 October 2026. These terms describe how Blueprint ("we", "us") works and what we expect from everyone who uses it.

1. Who can use Blueprint

  • You must be at least 16 years old. Blueprint does not knowingly allow accounts for anyone younger.
  • If you are under 18 (or under the age of adulthood where you live), you may only use Blueprint with the permission of a parent or guardian, who agrees to these terms on your behalf. By creating an account you confirm you have that permission.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and did not agree to your child using Blueprint, contact us and we will remove the account.
  • You are responsible for your account and password. Do not share your login.

2. Keeping young people safe

Safety comes first. Never:

  • post, share or ask for sexual content involving anyone under 18, or any content that exploits or endangers a child;
  • ask a young person for private details, photos, money, or to meet in person;
  • pretend to be younger or older than you are to get close to someone.

Messages are limited to people who follow each other by default, and anyone can block or report another person at any time. We act quickly on child safety reports. We remove the content, close accounts, and may report the case to the police or the child protection authorities where the law allows or requires it.

3. Your content

You own what you post. By posting, you give us permission to store, display and share it inside Blueprint so the service works. Only post videos, photos and music you made or have the right to use. If you add music, you confirm you are allowed to share it. Do not post other people's private information or images of them without their permission. Videos can be up to 20 minutes and live streams up to 2 hours.

4. Community rules

Do not post or send content that:

  • shows nudity or sexual acts;
  • threatens, harasses, bullies or targets people, or promotes hate against a group;
  • shows or encourages violence, self-harm, eating disorders or dangerous acts;
  • is a scam, impersonation, fake information or spam, or sells illegal goods;
  • breaks the law or someone else's copyright, trademark or privacy.

5. Live streams and messages

You are responsible for what you stream and say. Do not stream anyone who has not agreed to be filmed, and do not stream private places. Messages are private between the people in the chat but are not end-to-end encrypted, and we may review them when needed for safety, to answer a report, or by law.

6. Reports and enforcement

Use the Report button on posts, comments and profiles. We may remove content, limit features, suspend or ban accounts that break these rules. Serious or repeated breaches can lead to a permanent ban. If you think we made a mistake, contact us and we will look again.

7. Your account and data

You can delete your account in Settings at any time. This deletes your profile, posts, messages and followers. See the Privacy Policy for how we handle personal data and the rights you have.
