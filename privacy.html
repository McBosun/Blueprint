What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored securely by our login provider).
  • Profile: username, name, bio, photo, location, link and interests you choose to add.
  • Content: videos, photos, music, comments, likes, saves, follows and live streams you create.
  • Messages you send and receive.
  • Activity: which posts you watch and for how long (to recommend videos), and which profiles you view if you allow that.

How we use it

To run the app, show your content, deliver messages and notifications, recommend videos, keep people safe, and answer reports.

Who sees what

  • Posts, comments, profiles and live streams are public to other users.
  • Messages are visible only to the two people in the chat, and to the operators of the service when needed for safety or by law.
  • Profile viewers can see that you viewed their profile unless you turn that off in Settings.

Services we use

Blueprint is hosted on GitHub Pages and stores data with Supabase. Live video connects directly between phones and uses Google's public STUN servers to find routes. These providers process data for us.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, hide profile-view sharing, change who can message you, block and report people, and delete your account in Settings. Deleting your account removes your profile, posts, messages and followers.

Children

Blueprint is for people 16 and older. If you believe a younger child has an account, contact us and we will remove it.
