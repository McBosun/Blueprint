Blueprint Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026. This explains what personal data Blueprint ("we", "us") collects, why, who sees it, and the rights you have. It is written to follow the principles shared by most data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act and the EU/UK GDPR.

1. What we collect

  • Account: email address and password (the password is stored securely by our login provider, never in plain text). We also record that you accepted the terms and when.
  • Profile: username, name, bio, photo, location text, link and interests that you choose to add.
  • Content: videos, photos, music, comments, likes, saves, follows and live streams you create.
  • Messages you send and receive.
  • Activity: which posts you watch and for how long (used to recommend videos), and which profiles you view if you allow that.
  • Reports and safety records about accounts and content.
  • Device access you allow: camera and microphone only while you go live, and files you pick from your phone. We do not collect your precise location.

We collect only what is needed to run Blueprint. We do not sell personal data and we do not show advertising.

2. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To run your account and show your content (to provide the service you asked for).
  • To deliver messages and notifications (to provide the service).
  • To recommend videos (our legitimate interest in a useful app, and your consent where the law requires it).
  • To keep people safe, enforce our rules and answer reports (legitimate interest and legal duty).
  • To comply with the law and legal requests.

3. Children and young people

  • Blueprint is for people 16 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16, and we delete it when we find it.
  • People under 18 need the permission of a parent or guardian. We treat the data of young people with extra care: we collect no more than the app needs, we do not use it for advertising, and messages are limited to mutual followers by default.
  • A parent or guardian can contact us to ask what we hold about their child, to correct it, or to have the account and data deleted. We may ask for proof of identity or relationship before acting.
  • If you believe a child under 16 has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

4. Who sees your data

  • Your posts, comments, profile and live streams are visible to other users.
  • Messages are visible to the two people in the chat, and to the operators of the service when needed for safety, to handle a report, or by law.
  • Other people can see that you viewed their profile unless you turn that off in Settings.
  • We share data only with providers who help run Blueprint, and only as needed (see section 5), or when the law requires it.

5. Services we use

  • Supabase: stores accounts, content and messages, and sends live notifications.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app's pages.
  • Google STUN servers: help live video find a route between phones. They can see your network address.
  • A relay (TURN) provider, if switched on: passes live video when a direct route is blocked.
  • jsDelivr: delivers code libraries, so it can see your network address when the app loads.

These providers may process data outside your country. We rely on their security commitments and on contracts that protect your data where the law requires.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, posts, messages, followers and stored files. Safety records, such as reports about rule breaks, and short-term backups may be kept a little longer where needed for safety, legal duties or technical reasons.

7. Security

We protect data with encrypted connections, database access rules so people can only reach what they should, and limits on who can use admin tools. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong (you can edit your profile yourself in the app);
  • delete your data (use Delete my account in Settings, or contact us);
  • object to or limit how we use your data, and withdraw consent you gave;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • not be subject to decisions made only by automated means that seriously affect you. Our video recommendations only decide what you see first, nothing more.

Contact us to use these rights. We will reply within a reasonable time, and within the time your law sets. You can also complain to your data protection authority, such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, or the authority in the country where you live.

9. Local storage

Blueprint keeps your login session and a few preferences in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
