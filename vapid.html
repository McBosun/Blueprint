Phone alert keys

This page makes a fresh pair of keys for Blueprint phone alerts. It works on your own phone and sends nothing anywhere.

1. Public key (goes in config.js as vapidKey; safe to share)

2. Private key (goes only in Supabase secrets as VAPID_PRIVATE)

Keep the private key secret. Never put it in GitHub, config.js or a message to anyone. Paste it into Supabase right away, then close this page. Making new keys later means every phone has to turn alerts on again.
